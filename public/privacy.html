ai_navi privacy policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026 · Türkçe

This policy explains which personal data the ai_navi navigation app processes, why, who receives it and what your rights are under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the UK GDPR and Turkish data protection law (KVKK).

Controller and contact

The developer of ai_navi. For questions, requests and deletion: hsynaltunta@gmail.com

No account, no tracking

ai_navi does not ask you to create an account and does not collect your name, phone number or e-mail address. No advertising or analytics tools are used.

Data that stays on your phone

  • Location: used to show your position, find the nearest station and give directions. Your location history is not recorded and not sent to a server (other than the service requests listed below).
  • Vehicle data: vehicle type, capacity and consumption you enter; battery / fuel level, speed and vehicle identification number (VIN) read from an OBD adapter. Stored only on the phone and never sent anywhere.
  • Settings, recent destinations, home / work / favourite places, parking spot and last known location: stored only on the phone.

Road reports (accidents, roadworks, etc.)

When you send a road report, only the report type, your current location and the time are stored in ai_navi's Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore, EU servers) database. Your name, phone number or account are not sent. To limit abuse (too many reports, several votes on the same report) Firebase gives your phone an anonymous number; it is not written into the report and is kept only with your last sending time and your votes. Depending on its type, a report is shown on other users' maps for 1 to 24 hours; expired reports and votes are deleted completely. Sending reports is optional and we ask for your explicit consent before the first one (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)); abuse limiting is based on our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)). Police-check reports are not available in countries where they are not allowed (for example Germany, Switzerland, Austria and France).

Suggestions to the developer

Text you send with "Send a suggestion to the developer" is stored in the same Firebase database in a section only the developer can read. App version, phone model, Android version, vehicle name and OBD status are attached; your location is not. If you add your e-mail address it is used only to reply to you (Art. 6(1)(a)).

International transfers

Firebase (Google Ireland / Google LLC) and GitHub (GitHub Inc.) may process data outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States, under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and/or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. Data sent to these services is limited to what is listed above.

Permissions

  • Location: navigation and nearest station.
  • Nearby devices / Bluetooth: connecting to an OBD adapter.
  • Internet: maps, directions and data.
  • Notifications / foreground service: keeping navigation running with the screen off; commute notification.

Retention

Data on the phone is deleted when you uninstall the app. Road reports and votes are deleted at the latest 1 day after they expire. Suggestions are kept for at most 1 year after they have been handled, or deleted earlier on request.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify and erase your data, to restrict or object to processing, to data portability and to withdraw consent at any time (without affecting earlier processing). Write to the e-mail address above. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your data protection supervisory authority. ai_navi does not make automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

Children

ai_navi is intended for drivers; we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.
